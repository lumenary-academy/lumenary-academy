## What Lumenary Academy is

Lumenary Academy is an independent education project. It has three divisions, each with its own website (see [the divisions](../divisions/)):

- **[Lumenary Scientia](https://scientia.lumenary.academy/en/)** teaches the sciences: mathematics, statistics, computer science and programming languages, electrical engineering, robotics, chemistry, mechanical engineering and materials science, each from first steps to the frontier. Physics, biology and other sciences are pending. Scientia is free and open source, with no accounts, advertising or paid tiers.
- **[Lumenary Lingua](https://lingua.lumenary.academy/en/)** teaches languages, each through a language the learner already knows. Its first two courses, Spanish taught in English and English taught in Spanish, run from A1 to C2. Lingua is free and open source, with no accounts or advertising.
- **[Lumenary Prep](https://prep.lumenary.academy)** prepares learners for exams: admissions tests, graduate and professional school tests, English-language tests and professional licences. Prep is a paid service: each course has one price, paid once, and some lessons are free samples that anyone can open. Paying for Prep is never needed to use Scientia or Lingua.

This website, lumenary.academy, is the academy's front page. Each division describes itself in more detail on its own site; Lumenary Scientia's [about page](https://scientia.lumenary.academy/en/about/) explains how it teaches and how it checks its lessons.

## Built with AI, honestly

Much of the academy's content and code was drafted with the help of AI systems. AI is a collaborator, not an oracle.

Every lesson in Lumenary Scientia and Lumenary Lingua was drafted with AI assistance, and none has yet been reviewed by a human expert in its field: a scientist or educator for Scientia, a teacher of the language for Lingua. Each lesson says so. Lumenary Prep's courses are labelled as AI-drafted until a qualified person has reviewed them. We would rather say this plainly than claim a trustworthiness we have not yet earned; expert review is the most valuable help anyone can give (see [Contribute](../contribute/)).

The interface is in six languages. Every translation other than English is a machine-generated draft awaiting human review, and is labelled as such. The academy's pages, such as this one, are in English only for now.

## Christian inspiration, offered and never imposed

Lumenary Academy is inspired by historic Christian values. We say so openly, because transparency is one of them. These values shape *how* we build and *how* we treat learners:

- **Truthfulness**: we never claim a proof, test, review or translation that has not happened.
- **Wisdom and humility**: we label uncertainty, admit our limitations, and welcome correction.
- **Love of neighbour and human dignity**: every learner, everywhere, deserves patient, respectful teaching. We never use shame, manipulative streaks or public rankings.
- **Generosity**: Lumenary Scientia and Lumenary Lingua are free and openly licensed.
- **Stewardship**: the sites are light, work on inexpensive devices, and are cheap to host.
- **Intellectual honesty**: claims are justified by evidence and argument, never by authority.
- **Wonder at creation**: curiosity is worth cultivating for its own sake.
- **Service**: the project exists for its learners, not the other way round.

Christian inspiration is **never a prerequisite**. You do not need to share any belief to learn here, and nothing in the lessons depends on one. A few lessons in Scientia and Lingua contain a short, clearly marked *optional reflection*; it is collapsed by default, separate from the instruction, and can simply be skipped.

We represent people and traditions of every religious and non-religious background accurately and respectfully. **Truth is not determined by the identity of the person who discovers it.**
