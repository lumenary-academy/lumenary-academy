## Lumenary Scientia

No lesson in Lumenary Scientia has yet been reviewed by a human expert, so the most valuable help is a review: of the mathematics and science by someone who knows the field, of the teaching by a teacher, parent or education researcher, or of a translation by someone fluent in the language. Scientia also takes contributions to its software.

[Lumenary Scientia's contribute page](https://scientia.lumenary.academy/en/contribute/) explains how to review a lesson, translate, and work on the code. Its source is at [github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-scientia](https://github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-scientia).

## Lumenary Lingua

Every Lingua lesson was drafted with AI assistance and awaits review by a human teacher of the language. If you teach Spanish or English, or are a careful native speaker, you can help without touching any code: read a lesson as you would a textbook chapter for your students, and report mistakes in the language, explanations that are wrong or too hard for their level, and exercises that reject a correct answer.

[Lingua's CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-lingua/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains what to look for and how to report it, with or without GitHub. Its source is at [github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-lingua](https://github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-lingua).

## Lumenary Prep

Lumenary Prep is a paid service. Feedback on it is welcome.

## This website

This site, the academy's front page, is built on the design system and interface shared by every Lumenary site, [@lumenary/core](https://github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-core). Its source is at [github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-academy](https://github.com/lumenary-academy/lumenary-academy). Its interface is translated into Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili and Simplified Chinese as unreviewed machine drafts; if you are fluent in one of these languages, a review of those strings (in `src/i18n/messages`) is very welcome.

## Share feedback

If something is confusing, wrong, inaccessible or unwelcoming, we want to know. Clear, kind, specific feedback ("this step in the proof skips a case", "this example assumes a currency most of the world doesn't use") is a gift. For Scientia, Lingua and this site, open an issue in its repository.

## Ground rules

Be truthful, be kind, and be precise. Respect learners and contributors of every country, culture and belief. Never claim a review, test or translation that did not happen.
